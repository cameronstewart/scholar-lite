We are delighted to announce that **Professor Sarah Johnson** has been awarded the prestigious **National Science Foundation (NSF) CAREER Award**!

## About the Award

The NSF CAREER Award is one of the most prestigious recognitions for early-career faculty in the United States. It supports junior faculty who exemplify the role of teacher-scholars through outstanding research, excellent education, and the integration of both.

## Research Project

Professor Johnson's CAREER project, titled **"Building Trustworthy AI: Efficient and Interpretable Deep Learning for Critical Applications"**, aims to develop novel methods for creating AI systems that are both computationally efficient and inherently interpretable.

The research will focus on:
- Designing neural architectures that provide built-in interpretability
- Developing efficient training methods for large-scale models
- Creating evaluation frameworks for trustworthy AI
- Applying these methods to critical domains like healthcare and autonomous systems

## Educational Component

The project includes an innovative educational component that will:
- Develop new courses on responsible AI development
- Create hands-on workshops for K-12 students to learn about AI ethics
- Establish mentorship programs for underrepresented students in computer science
- Produce open-source educational materials for the broader community

The five-year, $550,000 grant will support multiple PhD students, undergraduate researchers, and outreach activities.

Congratulations, Professor Johnson, on this well-deserved recognition!
