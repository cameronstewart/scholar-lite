title: "New International Research Collaboration with MIT"
date: 2025-12-15
summary: "We are excited to announce a new research collaboration with MIT's Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (CSAIL)."
---
We are thrilled to announce a new international research collaboration with the prestigious Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (CSAIL) at MIT. This partnership will focus on advancing research in **multimodal learning** and **large-scale vision-language models**.

The collaboration will involve joint research projects, student exchanges, and co-authored publications. We look forward to combining our expertise with MIT's world-class research facilities to push the boundaries of AI research.

This partnership was made possible through a grant from the National Science Foundation's International Collaboration Program.
