title: "PhD Student Receives IEEE Outstanding Student Award"
date: 2025-11-05
summary: "Congratulations to Emily Chen for receiving the IEEE Computer Society Outstanding Student Award for her exceptional research contributions."
---
We are delighted to announce that **Emily Chen**, one of our talented PhD students, has been awarded the **IEEE Computer Society Outstanding Student Award** for 2025!

Emily's research on efficient vision transformers and their applications to medical image analysis has made significant contributions to the field. Her work has been published in top-tier conferences including CVPR, ICCV, and ECCV.

The IEEE Computer Society Outstanding Student Award recognizes exceptional students who have demonstrated outstanding academic achievement, leadership, and contributions to the field of computer science.

Congratulations, Emily! We are incredibly proud of your achievements and look forward to your continued success.
