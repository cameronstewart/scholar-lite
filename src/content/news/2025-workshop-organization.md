We are proud to announce that our lab will be organizing a workshop at the **International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV) 2025** in Paris, France.

The workshop, titled **"Efficient AI Systems: From Theory to Practice"**, will bring together researchers and practitioners to discuss the latest advances in efficient deep learning, model compression, neural architecture search, and edge AI deployment.

**Key Topics:**
- Neural network pruning and quantization
- Knowledge distillation
- Efficient transformer architectures
- Hardware-aware neural architecture search
- Edge AI and TinyML

The workshop will feature invited talks from leading researchers in the field, as well as a poster session for contributed papers. We encourage submissions from both academic and industrial researchers.

**Important Dates:**
- Paper submission deadline: August 15, 2025
- Notification of acceptance: September 10, 2025
- Workshop date: October 28, 2025
