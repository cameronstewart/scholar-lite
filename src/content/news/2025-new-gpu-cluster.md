We are thrilled to announce that our lab has received a new **high-performance GPU cluster** equipped with **128 NVIDIA H100 GPUs**! This significant upgrade will dramatically accelerate our research on large-scale foundation models and enable us to tackle more ambitious projects.

## Specifications

- **128 NVIDIA H100 80GB GPUs**
- **High-speed NVLink interconnects** for efficient multi-GPU training
- **2 PB of high-speed NVMe storage**
- **10 Gbps network connectivity** to the campus data center

## Impact on Research

This new infrastructure will enable us to:
- Train larger foundation models with billions of parameters
- Conduct more extensive hyperparameter searches
- Run larger-scale experiments for our vision-language research
- Support more students and projects simultaneously

We are grateful to the university and our sponsors for making this investment possible. The cluster will be operational starting September 1, 2025.

Special thanks to our systems administrator, John Chen, for overseeing the installation and configuration process!
