We are excited to announce the launch of our **Monthly AI Seminar Series**, starting this September! The seminar series will feature talks from distinguished researchers, industry leaders, and rising stars in the field of artificial intelligence and machine learning.

## Upcoming Seminars

**September 2025:** Dr. Lisa Wang (Stanford University)
*"Foundation Models for Scientific Discovery"*

**October 2025:** Dr. James Martinez (Google DeepMind)
*"Scaling Laws and the Future of Large Language Models"*

**November 2025:** Dr. Priya Sharma (Carnegie Mellon University)
*"Multimodal Learning: Bridging Vision and Language"*

## Format

Each seminar will consist of a 45-minute talk followed by a 15-minute Q&A session. Seminars will be held on the first Friday of each month at 3:00 PM in the department auditorium. Remote attendance will be available via Zoom for those unable to attend in person.

All seminars are open to students, faculty, and the broader research community. We encourage everyone to attend and participate in the discussions!

For more information and to receive seminar announcements, please subscribe to our mailing list.
