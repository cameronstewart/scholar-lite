Last week, several members of our lab attended the **Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS) 2025** in Vancouver, Canada. This year's conference was particularly exciting, with over 10,000 attendees from around the world.

## Our Presentations

We presented three papers at the conference:
- **"Scaling Foundation Models with Efficient Attention"** (Oral presentation)
- **"Multimodal Pretraining for Zero-Shot Learning"** (Spotlight presentation)
- **"Adversarial Robustness in Vision-Language Models"** (Poster)

Our oral presentation on efficient attention mechanisms for foundation models received significant interest from the community, leading to many fruitful discussions and potential collaborations.

## Highlights

The conference featured fascinating keynote talks on AI safety, the future of large language models, and the intersection of neuroscience and AI. We also had the opportunity to network with researchers from leading institutions and companies worldwide.

We returned from NeurIPS with new ideas, collaborations, and renewed energy for our research!
