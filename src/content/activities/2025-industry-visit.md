Last week, members of our lab had the incredible opportunity to visit **NVIDIA Research** headquarters in Santa Clara, California. The visit was organized as part of our ongoing collaboration on efficient AI systems and hardware-software co-design.

## Highlights of the Visit

### Technical Talks
We attended several fascinating technical presentations covering:
- The latest advances in GPU architecture for AI workloads
- NVIDIA's research on efficient transformer models
- Hardware acceleration techniques for vision-language models
- The future of AI infrastructure

### Lab Tours
We toured NVIDIA's state-of-the-art research facilities, including:
- The robotics lab showcasing autonomous systems
- The visualization lab with advanced VR/AR technology
- The AI systems lab with massive GPU clusters

### Research Discussions
We had productive discussions about potential collaborations on:
- Hardware-aware neural architecture search
- Efficient inference for foundation models
- Benchmarking AI systems across different hardware platforms

## Outcomes

The visit strengthened our partnership with NVIDIA Research and opened up new opportunities for collaboration. We discussed joint projects, internship opportunities for our students, and potential equipment donations to support our research.

Special thanks to the NVIDIA Research team for hosting us and for the engaging discussions!
