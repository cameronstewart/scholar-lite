Last weekend, our lab successfully hosted the **3rd Annual AI for Social Good Hackathon**, bringing together over 200 students from various universities to develop innovative AI solutions for pressing social challenges.

## Event Overview

The 48-hour hackathon challenged participants to develop AI applications addressing one of four themes:
- **Healthcare Access:** Improving medical diagnosis and treatment in underserved communities
- **Climate Action:** Using AI for environmental monitoring and conservation
- **Education Equity:** Creating AI-powered learning tools for diverse learners
- **Disaster Response:** Developing AI systems for emergency management and relief

## Winning Projects

### 🥇 First Place: MediAssist
A mobile app using computer vision to help community health workers diagnose skin conditions in rural areas with limited access to dermatologists.

### 🥈 Second Place: EcoWatch
An AI-powered satellite image analysis system for detecting illegal deforestation in real-time.

### 🥉 Third Place: LearnAdapt
An adaptive learning platform that personalizes educational content for students with different learning disabilities.
